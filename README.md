## 🌾 Project: AgriQueue — Smart Mandi Procurement Platform

> **AgriQueue** is an end-to-end digital slot booking, dynamic token queueing, and DBT tracking platform designed to eliminate long waiting times, ad-hoc crowding, and payment uncertainty at agricultural procurement centers (mandis/APMCs).

---

## 📌 1. Problem Statement & Context

Farmers in rural India face severe operational and financial friction during crop procurement seasons:
* **Long Waiting Times:** Farmers spend anywhere from 8 to 48 hours waiting at procurement centers without queue visibility.
* **Lack of Scheduling:** Walk-in congestion overwhelms weighbridges and quality assessment gates.
* **Information Asymmetry:** Zero real-time updates regarding daily center intake status or operational delays.
* **Tracking & Payment Uncertainty:** Lack of transparent, end-to-end tracking from grain assaying to final Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payment clearance.

## Solution
A digital platform that enables farmer registration, slot booking, real-time queue management, notifications, and procurement/payment tracking.

## Features
- Farmer Registration
- Slot Booking
- Live Queue Tracking
- SMS/App Notifications
- Procurement Status Tracking
- Payment Status Updates
